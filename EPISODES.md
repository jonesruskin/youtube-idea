# Gravity Dial — episode order

Upload in this order. Each episode's end teases the **next row**, so the order is locked once an episode is rendered
(the quality gate checks the teaser against this table: `cinema/tools/qa.py <ep> pre`).

**How to upload:** open the episode's folder in `renders/`. Everything you need is in it: `upload.mp4`, `title.txt`,
`description.txt`, `tags.txt`, `thumbnail.png`, `captions.srt`, `pinned_comment.txt`. The step-by-step checklist is
`UPLOAD.md`. After uploading, add the video to the playlist **Gravity Dial: The Universe, Simulated**, then set its end
screen to point at the previous episode.

## Rules for the list
- **Order is locked once an episode ships**, because each video's ending teases the next row. To change the order,
  re-render the teaser of the episode before the change.
- **Titles vary in form** (never "What if"): a stake ("…Is Coming"), a result ("Here's How Far It Got"), a clock
  ("Every Minute After…"), a ranking ("How Long Could You Survive…"). The final title and two A/B variants are chosen
  when the episode is rendered, and written to its `title.txt` and `UPLOAD.md`.
- **Every episode is one dial turned, then simulated.** The twist always comes from the simulation, not from opinion.
