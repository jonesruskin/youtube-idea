## Pass 5: CEO review of the delivered v4.1 (eyes)
The CEO found that the eye change made faces worse: one eye looked bigger than the other on every character, and eyes looked half-closed in some scenes. Both were true, and both slipped past the review because faces were only judged on small four-up sheets, never at full size against the previous version.

| Found | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Eyes look half-closed on calm faces | Pass 1 drew a skin-coloured upper lid with an ink line across 20% of every calm eye. In this flat style that reads as droopy, bored eyes, and a pupil looking up tucks under the line | Lids removed from every calm face. Lids remain only for blinks, real sleepiness and anger; calm vs. startled is carried by eye size, pupil size and brows |
| One eye bigger than the other | The far eye in a 3/4 view was drawn 18% narrower, switched on as a hard step past a small head turn. v4's 3/4 dialogue blocking made it near-constant, and the lid line made it obvious | Smooth, mild foreshortening (at most 10%, about 5% at a typical 3/4 turn); both eyes read as the same size |
| Blinks looked like fluttering, half-shut lids | Triangle-shaped blink: 4 of its 5 frames were partly closed | The animator's blink: one in-between frame, two frames shut, one in-between frame |
| Kai sleepy on the stairs and roof after the blackout; Mira sleepy on the roof and on Day 1 | Wrong direction choice | focused / cold / worried. New lint gate: no main character may be sleepy after "lights-out" (tested: it fails on a planted regression) |

New review step: `scripts/eyelab.ts` renders `EyeLab`, each main character's every expression at the three head turns and gazes the film uses, at close-up size. It is checked before any render, and again at full size on real shots (kitchen, street, TV, roof, stairs, apartment).
