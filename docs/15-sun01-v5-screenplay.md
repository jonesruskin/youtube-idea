## Removed shots (and why)
- freezer: only made sense in a power cut.
- radio-news: phones work.
- hosp-fading, dont, mum-gone: Mum's static fade contradicted working power and phones.
- day180-lights: no power failure, so nothing returns.
- hosp-walk, bringing, sixty-below, walk-fast, river: the river crossing is replaced by the Day 30 clinic reunion.
- beam-star: merged into beam-news.
- leaders, every-day, what-send, waiting: the Sun is not held hostage.
- needed-the-sun, everything, a-hundred-years, telephone, answered: the old telephone premise.
- send-time, same-minute, eight-eight: the sunrise no longer depends on our message.
