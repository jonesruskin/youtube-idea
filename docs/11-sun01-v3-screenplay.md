# 11 · File 001 v3 screenplay: "The Sun Went Out"

**Logline.** On a summer morning the Sun vanishes. Eight-year-old Mira, her brother Kai and the retired
astronomer upstairs, Mr. Ito, survive a year in the dark and work out, clue by clue, what happened. It
wasn't an accident. And the man who has been saving them is the reason it happened.

**Narrator.** **Mira Okafor, grown up**, telling it years later in the first person, past tense. She only
narrates what she saw or was told. The 1982 thread is never narrated until the reveal, because she wasn't
there; she only learned it "on the last night".

**Two timelines (the Maharaja device).**
- **The present** (Day 0 → Day 365) is always night after minute two.
- **1982** is a young astronomer alone in a mountain observatory, always shown at night.
- Shown without dates, they read as simultaneous. The reveal is wordless: in 1982 he walks outside and the
  Sun rises.
- Then his face match-cuts to old Mr. Ito.

## Scene-by-scene (cause → effect)
Each scene enters for a reason the viewer already heard and leaves with the reason for the next.

**0 · Cold open (1982, unlabeled).**
- A hand on a Morse key: tap … tap-tap … tap-tap-tap.
- A dish turns toward one bright star. The tune is hummed.
- → *Cut on a white flash to a sunny kitchen.*

**1 · The last morning (6:51 AM, the city).**
- N: "I was eight the summer the Sun went out."
- Mira draws a sun. N: "I remember the last morning exactly. I was drawing it."
- Kai: "Who draws at six in the morning?" Mira: "Artists."
- Biscuit eyes the toast. N: "Biscuit was planning a crime."
- → *N widens out:* "At that moment, half the world was in sunlight."
- **SYDNEY 3:51 PM.** A surfer catches the wave of her life.
- **TAMIL NADU 11:21 AM.** A farmer squints at the sky for rain.
- **MANILA 1:51 PM.** A boy runs for his bus; the doors close on his nose.
- Back in the kitchen, Biscuit strikes. Kai: "Biscuit, no!"
- **SNAP: darkness.** The beach, the paddy and the bus stop go dark in the same instant (three one-second cuts). N: "Then the Sun went out."

**2 · Breakfast under the stars.**
- Stars through the window at breakfast time. Mira: "Is it night again?" Kai: "It's seven in the morning."
- Three knocks. N: "Then someone knocked. Three times."
- Ito at the door, dressed, with a torch, a radio and a canvas bag. Ito: "Good morning. Shoes on, please. Both of you."
- N: "Mr. Ito from upstairs. He had a torch, a radio, and a bag he'd packed the night before."
- Ito: "And bring the dog. And the toast."
- N: "It took me a year to understand why." → **TITLE**.

**3 · Hour one: the street.** *(cause: Ito leads them out)*
- The whole street is looking up. N: "Outside, the whole street was looking up."
- A man: "It's an eclipse!" Ito: "Eclipses are polite. They leave after seven minutes."
- Ito: "Roof. Bring the telescope."
- Kai hauls the telescope up the stairwell; Mira climbs behind him.

**4 · Hour one: the roof (twist 1).** *(cause: Ito wants to see one thing)*
- Ito: "Venus first." On the eyepiece, Venus is still shining. Mira: "Why is that one still on?"
- Diagram (the Sun as a dark "?"). Ito: "Venus shines with the Sun's light. That light takes the long way round to reach us."
- Ito: "If something happened at the Sun, Venus goes dark at four minutes past seven." (v3.1: Venus sits beside the dark Sun, so its light arrives ~12 min late; they reach the roof by ~7:00.)
- They wait. Biscuit yawns. Mira on Kai's lap.
- Kai checks his watch (callout 7:03). Venus winks out (callout 7:04).
- Ito, quiet: "Then it happened at the Sun." **TWIST 1.**
- Ito: "And Jupiter will go out at four minutes past eight." N: "At four minutes past eight, it did."
- Ito taps the telescope three times, humming. N: "He always tapped it three times. I never asked why."

**5 · 1982 #1.**
- A young man at a light table, magnifier over an old eclipse plate.
- A straight line, too straight. He circles it in red. Hums.

**6 · Morning: the cold and the radio.** *(cause: the temperature is falling)*
- The watch: 21.4 → 21.1 °C. N: "By nine, it was a degree colder."
- Radio: "…phone networks are down across the city. Stay indoors."
- Kai: "Everyone's going to the shops." Ito: "Then so are we."

**7 · Day 1: the supermarket (comedy).**
- N: "Kai was right. The whole city had the same idea."
- Kai reads Ito's list: "Candles. Rice. Batteries. Water."
- Mira arrives with dog treats: "It's on my list."
- Two men fight over the last heater. Ito: "Heaters need electricity. They'll learn."

**8 · Day 2: Mum, and the tide (twist 2).** *(cause: Kai asks the question)*
- Radio, Mum: "Kai, Mira, it's Mum. They need me at the hospital. Stay with Mr. Ito."
- N: "Mum was a nurse. She'd been on a night shift."
- Kai: "Is the Sun… dead?"
- Ito: "If it were gone, the tide would be a third lower. Go and look." He gives Kai a tide table.
- Diagram: Moon pull plus the hidden Sun's pull.
- Harbour, at the gauge post with a torch. N: "So Kai and I went to the harbour."
- Mira: "It's exactly on the line!" Kai: "So the Sun's still there."
- Mira: "Then where is it?" N: "The Sun hadn't gone anywhere. Something was in front of it." **TWIST 2.**
- Ice skins the water. Mira: "The sea's growing a skin." A fish passes under it.

**9 · Day 3: the aurora.** *(cause: a storm the Sun launched before it vanished)*
- From the roof, the sky ripples green. Radio: "…a solar storm that left the Sun four days ago."
- Mira: "It's saying goodbye."

**10 · 1982 #2.**
- He lays three plates side by side. Red lines, extended, meet at one point on a star chart.
- He writes **22** beside a faint star and taps the chart three times.

**11 · Day 7: the grid dies.** *(cause: the cold)*
- The lights die. N: "On Day 7, the power went out."
- Ito: "Fill the bath. Before the pipes freeze."
- Blankets go up. N: "Mr. Ito showed us how to make one warm room."
- Balcony freezer. Kai: "Free fridge. Finally, some good news."
- Candlelight. N: "Kai pretended he wasn't scared. He was holding my hand too hard."
- Smoke under the door of 3B. Ito: "They're burning furniture. Indoors."
- Ito: "Everyone out. The smoke you can see. The gas you can't." Evacuation.
- The street door bangs open; Biscuit bolts. Mira: "BISCUIT!" — **mid-roll**.

**12 · Day 8: the search.** *(cause: Biscuit is gone)*
- Kai alone with a torch: "Biscuit! Come on, boy." N: "Kai searched all night. Then again, it was always night now."
- A distant bark, **WOOF!** Paw prints. N: "They led to the only warm thing on the street."
- Biscuit is curled against the metro vent. Kai: "You stupid, brilliant dog."
- Diagram, ground heat. N: "A few metres down, the ground stays around ten degrees."
- N: "Biscuit hadn't run away. He'd found the way down."
- Piggyback down the stairs. N: "By morning, half the street had followed him underground."

**13 · Day 30: the station town.** *(cause: everyone moved underground)*
- N: "By Day 30, Harbour Street station was a town."
- Pink grow lamps.
- The big lamps come up at seven. Ito: "We'll call it sunrise."
- Mira teaches the little ones, all visibly younger and smaller. Mira: "It's yellow. And round. And it's warm on your face."
- A small kid holds up a grey sun. N: "Some of them had already forgotten what colour it was."
- Biscuit's fan club. N: "Biscuit got a fan club. And a bigger belly."

**14 · 1982 #3.**
- A rejection letter under the lamp ("…cannot support further observations…").
- On the rotary phone, a colleague laughs.
- He looks out at the dish.

**15 · Day 40: the camera (twist 3, the shell).** *(cause: Ito's project)*
- The workshop. N: "Mr. Ito spent weeks rebuilding a firefighter's camera." Ito: "It sees heat, not light."
- The surface. N: "On Day 40, he took us up to the surface."
- He aims at where the Sun should be. Ito: "It's warm." Ito: "And it's twice as wide as the Sun."
- **First sight of the shell.** N: "There was something around the Sun." Callout **A SHELL**.

**16 · Day 40: "Nobody did" (twist 4).** *(cause: the shell raises the question)*
- Back below. Mira: "Who built it?" Kai: "Nobody could build that in a day." Ito: "No. Nobody did."
- Ito takes a photo out of the canvas bag. Ito: "Nineteen-nineteen. Look at the lines."
- Blinds diagram. Ito: "It was always there. Edge-on, like the slats of a blind."
- Ito: "And on the morning the Sun went out, someone closed it." Callout **CLOSED**. **TWIST 4.**
- Mira: "You knew about the lines?" Ito: "I saw them once. A long time ago. Nobody listened."
- N: "I believed him. It was even true."

**17 · Day 100–180: the dark, and the light.** *(cause: the station runs on generators)*
- The lamps die. N: "On Day 100, the generators failed."
- Ito with the engineers: "I know a little about old machines."
- The coldest night. Kai, cracking: "How does this end, Mira?" Mira: "With the lights coming back on."
- **Day 180:** the lamps blaze. N: "On Day 180, they did."
- Mum walks in. N: "And so did Mum." The family reunites.
- Mum to Ito: "You kept them alive." Ito: "They kept me alive."
- Ito at his bench with wires and a dish. Mira: "What are you building?" Ito: "An ear."

**18 · Day 300: the thread (twist 5).** *(cause: the bigger lens)*
- N: "By Day 300, his camera had a much bigger lens."
- A thin, warm thread is leaving the shell. Beam map: it rises straight out of the planets' plane.
- Ito: "It's sending the light away." Kai: "Where?"
- The thread ends at one faint star. Ito, barely: "Twenty-two."
- N: "He whispered a number. Twenty-two. I didn't ask what it meant." **TWIST 5.**

**19 · 1982 #4.**
- His finger hovers over the key. He looks at "22" on the chart.
- He presses: tap. (Cut away.)

**20 · Day 365: the birthday.** *(cause: one year, and Ito's ear is finished)*
- Everyone climbs to the surface. Ito: "I need to point this at the Sun." N: "On Day 365, we went up together."
- The Milky Way. Mira: "One whole lap around the Sun. Happy birthday, Earth."

**21 · Day 365: the count (twist 6).**
- Ito's receiver clicks. Ito: "It's coming from the Sun."
- Pulses: one… two… three… then silence. They repeat.
- Diagram: Kai: "Mercury. Venus. Earth." Kai: "It stopped at three." Mira: "That's us." **TWIST 6.**

**22 · The turn (twist 7).**
- Ito: "No. It isn't counting planets."
- **1982:** young Ito taps 1… 2… 3, the same rhythm as the pulses.
- He walks out of the dome and **the Sun rises** over the mountains: the first sunrise since minute two.
- His face in the sunlight; he taps the rail three times. **Match cut** to old Ito's face in starlight, tapping his cane three times.
- N: "The man in the observatory was Mr. Ito. Forty-four years ago."
- Star chart. N: "He found the lines, and the star they pointed to."
- N: "And he knocked. One. Two. Three."
- Diagram: Earth → star, 22 ly; 1982 → 2004 → 2026. N: "Twenty-two years to get there. Twenty-two to come back."
- Ito: "It's been knocking back since the morning the Sun went out. I'm the only one who knew where to listen."
- Ito: "It isn't counting. It's answering." Callout **IT'S ANSWERING**. **TWIST 7.**
- Ito to Mira: "I'm sorry." Mira takes his hand.

**23 · Coda.**
- N: "That night, Mr. Ito told the world. Every radio on Earth heard it."
- The surfer, the farmer and the bus boy, each in the dark, each listening.
- Flashback, a summer dawn before it all: Ito alone on the roof watching the sunrise. N: "Every morning for forty-four years, Mr. Ito watched the sunrise."
- N: "I used to think it was because he liked it." Cut to black.
- End screen: N: "Whoever answered is still out there. What would you say back?" Then **The Reply · coming soon**.

## Cold-viewer test (a new viewer, from any episode)
- **Logline in one breath:** the Sun vanished, a girl and her brother survive a year, and the old man saving them is the reason. ✔
- **First 30 s:** who is talking (adult Mira), where (a kitchen), when (the last sunny morning), and what's wrong (the Sun goes out). ✔
- **Ending:** this episode's mystery (what happened, and why) is fully resolved. The open question (who answered, and what do they want) is the series spine. ✔


## v3.1 frame-audit changes (summary)
A 3-frames-per-shot audit of the v3 render found 39 problems. Fixes, by kind:
- **Staging:**
  - Mira's face no longer sits behind the table.
  - Both kids are at the door.
  - The street crowd looks up.
  - A real stair climb.
  - The whole floor evacuates.
  - Mira is present on Day 300 and watching the three taps, so "I didn't ask" and "I never asked" are her own memories.
- **Payoffs shown, not just said:**
  - the drawing, the fish under the ice, the held hand
  - the predicted tide mark, the lamp bank, the lettuce menu, the fat Biscuit
  - the camera parts, the long lens
  - the receiver at young Ito's ear, the plates and lines on his wall, the three rail taps at dawn
- **Logic:**
  - Venus at 7:04 (see audit).
  - The radio announcer introduces Mum's message.
  - On Day 365 the world is frozen (the surfer's sea, the farmer's field, the bus stop).
- **Clarity:** labels on every diagram body, VENUS labelled in the sky, the 7:03 to 7:04 clock.
- **Ending:** a memory look (warm film, gate weave) and one shot that dissolves through 44 years of sunrises: young, middle-aged, then old Ito tapping the telescope 1-2-3. It is tagged "EVERY MORNING · 1982–2026", so it can't be misread as the Sun coming back.
- **Hook:** the blackout lands at 0:26.7 (was 0:29.7) and is now an event: the dark sweeps across the room from the window.
